Classification Methodology

How ReviewSense calculates polarity

1. Text Cleaning: Strips markup, normalizes whitespace, standardizes punctuation.

2. Vectorization: Sublinear TF-IDF scaling over unigram and bigram word pairings.

3. Logistic Regression: Balanced class weights ensure minority classes are not penalized.

4. Probabilities: Softmax function outputs calibrated confidence across Positive, Neutral, and Negative.

Confidence Interpretation Guide

Understanding model score bands
High Confidence (> 65%): Unambiguous positive or negative sentiment signals.
Moderate Confidence (40% - 65%): Neutral feedback, balanced pros/cons, or subtle opinions.
Low Margin (< 40% plurality): Mixed opinions or short text without strong sentiment tokens.

Model Specifications & Limitations

Architectural facts and operational boundaries

Pipeline: TF-IDF unigram + bigram feature extraction with sublinear term frequency scaling, followed by balanced L2-regularized Logistic Regression.

Version: v1.2-tfidf-logreg

Dataset: Stratified customer review dataset

Operational Note: This model is intended for review feedback triage and sentiment trend analysis. Reviews with heavy sarcasm, domain-specific acronyms, or contradictory compound clauses may exhibit lower confidence.
